Who we are

A yarn specialist, from fiber to finished yarn.

Jiangsu Suyuan Baiyi Trading Co., Ltd. (SOIPOI) is headquartered in Zhangjiagang, Jiangsu — one of China's most mature textile regions — where we develop, design and manufacture yarns and fabrics for international markets.

We operate three wholly-owned mills (fancy yarn, core-spun yarn and worsted/woolen), four joint-venture enterprises and a network of more than 100 long-term partner production bases. As a first-tier agent for major acrylic-fiber producers, we control our raw-material supply from the source — giving customers dependable quality and sharper pricing.

Every year we deliver over 20,000 tons of yarn to brands and manufacturers across North America, Europe and Asia, supported by an R&D partnership with Donghua University and factories audited by multiple international brands.
